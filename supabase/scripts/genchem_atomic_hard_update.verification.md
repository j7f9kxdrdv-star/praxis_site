## 10 — Periodic Trends — Skill 2 (id fcf88274)

- D. The \(\text{Mg} \rightarrow \text{Al}\) break, because aluminum's \(3p\) electron lies in a higher-energy, less penetrating subshell than magnesium's filled \(3s\), so it is held less tightly even with no paired electron.
