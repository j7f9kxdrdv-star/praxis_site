## Q1 — Disproportionation Reactions — easy — Skill 1 — 4E Stoichiometry (GC) (g48)

This reaction is classified as a disproportionation. Which statement best identifies the feature of this reaction that makes it a disproportionation?

- A. Copper acts only as a spectator ion and its oxidation state remains +1 throughout the reaction.
- B. Two different elements exchange electrons, with copper being oxidized while a separate element is reduced.
- C. Copper is reduced from +2 to +1 while no atom in the reaction is oxidized.

**Correct D**
