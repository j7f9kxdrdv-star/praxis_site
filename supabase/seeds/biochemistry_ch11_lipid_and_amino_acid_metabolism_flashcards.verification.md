## Disagreements with corpus

**One material misleading framing in the source materials.** The chapter outline (.docx para 167) describes ketolysis as "acetoacetate is oxidized to acetoacetyl-CoA." This is misleading — the chemistry is actually a CoA *transfer* from succinyl-CoA to acetoacetate (catalyzed by SCOT), which is an activation reaction, not an oxidation. The oxidation state of the carbon skeleton does not change during this step. Card 59 corrects the framing using the standard activation terminology per LibreTexts.

The three confidence-4 cards (27 HDL → steroidogenic tissues, 66 body protein catabolism only under starvation, 70 basic AAs feed urea cycle) all use established MCAT-prep framings that are widely taught but covered with somewhat looser language in introductory textbooks.
