## Disagreements with corpus

The source materials contained one notable framing disagreement: the .docx and the .apkg each listed a different "three antibody effector functions" triad — the .docx had neutralization / opsonization / agglutination, while the .apkg had neutralization / opsonization / complement activation. Per the textbook framing (OpenStax A&P 2e §21.4 and OpenStax Biology 2e §42.3), all four mechanisms — neutralization, opsonization, agglutination, and complement activation — are parallel antibody effector functions. The deck dedicates one card to each (cards 22–25) rather than enforcing an artificial three-item list.

The four confidence-4 cards (14, 31, 61, 66) use standard biochemistry terminology that is firmly established in the discipline but lightly framed in OpenStax (transport-vs-sequestration binding modes; transport Tmax saturation kinetics; loss of disulfide/salt bridge positions during primary-structure analysis; Bradford assay disrupted by detergents and excess buffer). Framing was kept conservative and consistent with the corpus.
