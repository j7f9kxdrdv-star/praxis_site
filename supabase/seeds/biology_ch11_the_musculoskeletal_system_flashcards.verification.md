## Disagreements with corpus

**Two material factual errors in the source materials.** The chapter outline (.docx para 33) describes sympathetic stimulation of the heart as "Neonephrine from sympathetic neurons or epinephrine from the adrenal medulla binds to androgenic receptors in the heart." Two errors: (1) "Neonephrine" → norepinephrine (autocorrect); (2) "androgenic receptors" → adrenergic receptors. Androgenic receptors are receptors for male sex hormones (a completely different organ system) — they have nothing to do with cardiac sympathetic control. Corrected per OpenStax A&P 2e §15.1 and §19.2. Card 14 reflects the corrected fact (NE/Epi → adrenergic receptors → increased rate and contractility).

The chapter outline (.docx para 23) also describes cardiac muscle as "primarily unnucleated" — should be "uninucleated" (one nucleus, not zero). Corrected in Card 10.

The three confidence-4 cards (63 vitamin D net effect framing, 74 rotation terminology) use widely-accepted MCAT-prep framings that are firmly established but presented with light coverage in OpenStax's introductory framing.
