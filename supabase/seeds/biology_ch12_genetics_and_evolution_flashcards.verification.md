## Disagreements with corpus

**One material factual disagreement in the source materials.** The chapter outline (.docx para 64) defines insertion as "Insertion mutations occur when a segment of DNA is moved from one chromosome to another" — but that is the definition of translocation, not insertion. At the chromosomal level, insertion means adding a segment to a chromosome (regardless of origin), distinct from translocation's specific swap-between-chromosomes mechanism. Corrected per OpenStax Biology 2e §17.3. Card 29 pairs the corrected insertion definition with the correct translocation definition using parallel-cloze treatment.

**One internally contradictory passage** in the chapter outline (paras 21-24 on Huntington's disease penetrance): para 22 claims "Individuals with fewer than 40 sequence repeats show high penetrance" while para 24 (just two paragraphs later) correctly states "With fewer sequence repeats, the gene comes to have reduced penetrance." Standard clinical genetics: the 36-39 CAG repeat range is the reduced-penetrance zone, not high-penetrance. Card 13 uses only the well-established full-penetrance threshold (≥40 repeats) to avoid teaching the contradiction.

The three confidence-4 cards (37 genetic-leakage terminology, 68 parallel evolution as separate category, 70 molecular clock model) use AAMC-MCAT prep framings that are widely accepted but covered lightly in OpenStax's introductory treatment.
