## Notes / editorial decisions
- **Concept summary was partially illegible** — the Molarity/Molality/Normality definitions were obscured by glare in the source photo (noted in the summary itself). Not blocking: the terms were still listed, the Anki deck defined all three, and prose is sourced from OpenStax regardless.
- **Source Anki defects corrected:** garbled Ksp/ion-product expressions (ASCII carets, inconsistent exponents) — restated so the ion product has the same algebraic form as Ksp but uses actual, non-equilibrium concentrations; ASCII multiplication (`i x Kb x m`) converted to proper Unicode; over-blanked solubility-rule exception lists (4–5 blanks in one group) split.
- **Kf disambiguated:** the source deck used `Kf` for BOTH the complex-ion formation (stability) constant and the freezing-point-depression constant. Every card naming Kf states which one; the assembler hard-fails a bare ambiguous `Kf`.
- **Cross-deck boundaries enforced:** electrolytes left to Ch 4; Henry's law and the gas-mixture mole-fraction formula left to Ch 8 (mole fraction appears here only as a concentration unit / for Raoult's law); Le Chatelier and general Keq machinery left to Ch 6 (its reasoning is used, not taught); pH/pKa math left to Ch 10 (only the qualitative solubility-pH link is here). Ch 6 Equilibrium was verified to contain **zero** Ksp/solubility content, so solubility equilibria belongs here.
- **AAMC gaps filled:** 'solubility and pH' (an explicit AAMC bullet absent from both the concept summary and the Ch 10 deck) and the common-ion effect's 'use in laboratory separations' (selective precipitation).
- **Known overlap, kept deliberately:** the Biochem Ch 8 Membranes deck also carries an osmotic-pressure (π = iMRT) and a colligative-definition card. Colligative properties are AAMC-tagged (GC), so their canonical home is this deck; the overlap is cross-subject and the cards schedule independently.
