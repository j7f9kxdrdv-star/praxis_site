## Disagreements with corpus

**One material factual disagreement in the source materials.** The chapter outline (.docx para 25) states that ionic compounds form a crystalline lattice in which "the attractive forces between oppositely charged ions are maximized, and the repulsive forces between ions of like charges are **maximized**." This is internally contradictory and incorrect — the actual physical principle is that the lattice geometry maximizes attraction while minimizing repulsion (that's what makes the lattice the lowest-energy state and what gives ionic compounds their high melting points). Corrected per OpenStax Chemistry: Atoms First 2e §4.1. Card 11 reflects the correct fact.

The two confidence-4 cards (8 EN cutoff at 1.7, 60 water-vs-methane H-bond comparison) use the AAMC-standard MCAT framings rather than the broader chemistry literature, which sometimes uses slightly different cutoffs or examples.
