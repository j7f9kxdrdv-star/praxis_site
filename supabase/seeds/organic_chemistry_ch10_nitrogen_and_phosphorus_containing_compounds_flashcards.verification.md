## Disagreements with corpus

**One material factual disagreement** in the source materials. The chapter outline (.docx para 17) and Anki note 17 both list tryptophan and phenylalanine as "nonpolar nonaromatic" amino acids — they are actually **aromatic** amino acids (containing benzene-derived ring systems). The correct nonpolar nonaromatic list is alanine, valine, leucine, isoleucine, glycine, proline, and methionine. Corrected per LibreTexts Biochemistry (Card 8). The .apkg note 18 has the aromatic list correct.

**One material mechanistic garble** in .docx para 39 (Strecker step 2/3): "The nitrile oxygen on the aminonitrile it's protonated... attacks the nitrocarbon" — nitriles contain a carbon-nitrogen triple bond (C≡N) and have no oxygen at all; "nitrocarbon" is an autocorrect for "nitrile carbon." Card 25 supplies the correct mechanism (water hydrolyzes the nitrile to a carboxylic acid via amide intermediate, simplified for MCAT presentation).

The two confidence-4 cards (42 intracellular phosphate buffer role, 48 PPᵢ hydrolysis driving DNA polymerization forward) reference well-established biochemical mechanisms that are nonetheless covered with lighter depth in introductory organic chemistry textbooks.
