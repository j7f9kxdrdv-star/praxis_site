## Scientific accuracy verification
- ✅ Alcohol formula ROH; -OH = hydroxyl — LibreTexts Organic
- ✅ IUPAC -ol suffix; hydroxy- prefix when secondary priority — LibreTexts
- ✅ Phenol = benzene + OH; phenol acidity > ordinary alcohol — LibreTexts
- ✅ ortho (1,2-), meta (1,3-), para (1,4-) positions — standard
- ✅ Alcohols H-bond → higher BP than analogous hydrocarbons — OpenStax
- ✅ More -OH groups → more H-bonding → higher BP — verified
- ✅ H-bonding requires H bonded to N, O, or F — standard
- ✅ Phenoxide resonance delocalization across aromatic ring → acidity — LibreTexts
- ✅ EWG ↑ acidity by stabilizing conjugate base; EDG ↓ acidity — LibreTexts
- ✅ Alkyl groups stabilize cations (inductive donation), destabilize anions — verified
- ✅ Diol > monoalcohol > alkane BP ranking via H-bonding count; chain length adds VdW — verified
- ✅ PCC (pyridinium chlorochromate) = mild, anhydrous, Cr(VI)-based oxidant — LibreTexts
- ✅ PCC: 1° alcohol → aldehyde only (stops there) — verified
- ✅ Mechanism: anhydrous → no geminal diol → no overoxidation to carboxylic acid — LibreTexts
- ✅ PCC + 2° alcohol → ketone — verified
- ✅ Tertiary alcohols cannot be oxidized without C–C cleavage — LibreTexts
- ✅ Jones oxidation: CrO₃ + dilute H₂SO₄ + acetone; 1° → carboxylic acid; 2° → ketone — LibreTexts
- ✅ Mesylate –OSO₂CH₃ from MsCl; tosylate –OSO₂C₆H₄CH₃ from TsCl — LibreTexts
- ✅ Mesylates and tosylates as better leaving groups than alcohols themselves — LibreTexts
- ✅ Acetal from aldehyde + 2 ROH; ketal from ketone + 2 ROH — LibreTexts
- ✅ Aqueous acid deprotection of acetals/ketals back to carbonyls — LibreTexts
- ✅ Quinones from phenol oxidation; cyclic diones; resonance-stabilized electrophiles — LibreTexts
- ✅ Quinones in ETC (aerobic respiration + photosynthesis) — OpenStax Biology
- ✅ Vitamin K₁ (phylloquinone): plant/green leafy source; K₂ (menaquinone): gut bacterial + animal source — verified
- ✅ Vitamin K functions: blood clotting (γ-carboxylation of clotting factors) + bone health — OpenStax
- ✅ Hydroxyquinones: -OH substituents make them less electrophilic via EDG donation — LibreTexts
- ✅ Ubiquinone = Coenzyme Q = electron carrier for ETC Complexes I/II/III — OpenStax Biology
