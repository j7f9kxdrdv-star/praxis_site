## Scientific accuracy verification
- ✅ Aldehyde = terminal carbonyl + at least one H; ketone = internal carbonyl + two alkyl groups — LibreTexts
- ✅ IUPAC suffixes: -al (aldehyde), -one (ketone); prefixes: oxo-, keto-; ring aldehydes: -carbaldehyde — LibreTexts
- ✅ C=O polarity → δ+ on carbon → electrophilic — LibreTexts
- ✅ BP hierarchy alcohol > carbonyl > alkane (H-bond > dipole > VdW) — verified
- ✅ Aldehyde > ketone reactivity via reduced steric hindrance + fewer EDG alkyls stabilizing δ+ — LibreTexts
- ✅ 1° alcohol → aldehyde with PCC; stronger oxidizers overoxidize to carboxylic acid — LibreTexts
- ✅ 2° alcohol → ketone with multiple oxidizers (PCC, Na₂Cr₂O₇, CrO₃, KMnO₄) — verified
- ✅ Nucleophilic addition mechanism: nucleophile attacks carbonyl C, π electrons go to O → alkoxide — LibreTexts
- ✅ No LG case: alkoxide protonated → alcohol (carbonyl cannot reform) — verified
- ✅ Good LG case: carbonyl reforms, LG kicks off → nucleophilic acyl substitution (foreshadows carboxylic acid derivative chemistry) — LibreTexts
- ✅ Hydration → geminal diol with water as nucleophile — LibreTexts
- ✅ 1 eq alcohol → hemiacetal (from aldehyde) or hemiketal (from ketone) — verified
- ✅ 2 eq alcohol → acetal/ketal via anhydrous acid SN1 mechanism — LibreTexts
- ✅ Mechanism: hemiacetal -OH protonated → loss of water → oxocarbenium ion → second alcohol attacks — LibreTexts
- ✅ Acetals/ketals as protecting groups; aqueous acid + heat for deprotection — verified
- ✅ Imine (C=N) formation from NH₃ or primary amine + carbonyl with loss of water — LibreTexts
- ✅ Hydroxylamine → oxime; hydrazine → hydrazone; semicarbazide → semicarbazone; primary amine → imine — verified
- ✅ Imine ↔ enamine tautomerization (analogous to keto-enol) — LibreTexts
- ✅ HCN (pKa ~9.2) → CN⁻ nucleophile → cyanohydrin (–OH and –CN on same C) — LibreTexts
- ✅ Aldehydes oxidized to carboxylic acids by any oxidizer stronger than PCC — verified
- ✅ Common aldehyde oxidizers: KMnO₄, CrO₃, Ag₂O (Tollens' reagent precursor), H₂O₂ — LibreTexts
- ✅ Ketones cannot be further oxidized without C–C cleavage — verified
- ✅ Hydride reduction: aldehyde → 1° alcohol; ketone → 2° alcohol — verified
- ✅ LiAlH₄ strong (handles carboxylic acids, esters, amides + aldehydes/ketones); NaBH₄ mild (aldehydes/ketones only) — LibreTexts (cross-references OrgChem Ch 4 card 30)
