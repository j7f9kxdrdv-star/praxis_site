## Scientific accuracy verification
- ✅ –COOH = carbonyl + hydroxyl on same C; always terminal — LibreTexts
- ✅ IUPAC suffixes -oic acid / -carboxylic acid (ring-attached) / -dioic acid (diacid) — LibreTexts
- ✅ Salt naming with cation first + -oate suffix — verified
- ✅ Carboxylic acids hydrogen bond via both –OH and C=O (donor + acceptor) — LibreTexts
- ✅ Dimer formation via two H-bonds → BPs exceed alcohols of similar size — LibreTexts
- ✅ Acidity from carboxylate resonance (negative charge across both oxygens) — LibreTexts
- ✅ EWG ↑ acidity by stabilizing conjugate base; EDG ↓ acidity — LibreTexts
- ✅ Proximity rule: closer substituent has greater effect — verified
- ✅ Dicarboxylic acids more acidic than monocarboxylic; first deprotonation lowers second acidity — LibreTexts
- ✅ β-dicarboxylic acid α-H extreme acidity via stabilization by both EWG carboxyls — LibreTexts
- ✅ Synthesis: 1° alcohol or aldehyde oxidation with Na₂Cr₂O₇, CrO₃, KMnO₄ — verified
- ✅ Nucleophilic acyl substitution mechanism: tetrahedral intermediate → carbonyl reforms + LG departs — LibreTexts
- ✅ Five acyl derivatives (CA, ester, amide, anhydride, acyl halide) — verified
- ✅ Amide formation from carboxylic acid + ammonia/amine; either acidic or basic conditions — LibreTexts
- ✅ Amide naming with -amide suffix and N-alkyl prefix; lactams = cyclic amides — LibreTexts
- ✅ Amide resonance: N lone pair → C=O, gives C–N partial double-bond character, restricts rotation — LibreTexts (foundational for protein peptide bond)
- ✅ Fischer esterification: CA + alcohol + acid catalyst → ester + water — LibreTexts
- ✅ Acid catalyst protonates carbonyl O → ↑ carbonyl C electrophilicity — LibreTexts
- ✅ Ester naming: alkyl from alcohol + acid name with -oate; lactones = cyclic esters — verified
- ✅ Anhydride formation from condensation of two CAs with loss of water — LibreTexts
- ✅ LiAlH₄ reduces CA → 1° alcohol via aldehyde intermediate; NaBH₄ cannot — LibreTexts (cross-references Ch 4, 5, 6, 7)
- ✅ Decarboxylation = loss of –COOH as CO₂ → replaced by H — LibreTexts
- ✅ β-keto acids and β-dicarboxylic acids decarboxylate readily via 6-membered cyclic TS; enol intermediate tautomerizes to keto — verified
- ✅ Saponification: fatty acid + NaOH/KOH → soap (carboxylate salt) — LibreTexts
- ✅ Soap amphipathicity: polar carboxylate head + nonpolar hydrocarbon tail — verified
- ✅ Micelle structure: polar heads outward, nonpolar tails inward; dissolves grease — verified
