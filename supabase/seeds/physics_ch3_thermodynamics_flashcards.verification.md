## Notes / editorial decisions
- **Cross-subject overlap with Chemistry Ch 7 Thermochemistry (deliberate):** the shared fundamentals (zeroth/first/second laws, systems, state functions, specific heat, entropy) appear in both decks. They are framed for PHYSICS here — the first law uses ΔU = Q − W (work done BY the system, the opposite sign from chem's q + w), specific heat is given in cal, and the deck emphasizes the physics-only content. This is accepted cross-subject overlap (like colligative properties across Solutions/Membranes); the two decks schedule independently and serve different exam sections.
- **Physics-only content (the bulk of the deck):** thermal expansion (ΔL = αL₀ΔT, ΔV = βV₀ΔT), the four thermodynamic processes (isothermal/adiabatic/isobaric/isochoric), work done by a gas W = PΔV and as the area under a PV curve, and the three heat-transfer modes (conduction/convection/radiation).
- **Glare gap filled:** the source summary's Second-Law section was photo-degraded; the entropy equation ΔS = Q_rev/T was rebuilt from OpenStax.
- **Chemistry-only content left to Ch 7:** enthalpy of formation, Hess's law, bond dissociation energies, the Gibbs-free-energy spontaneity criterion, and coffee-cup/bomb calorimeter types.
- **Notation:** plain Unicode physics throughout — Δ, proper minus −, subscripts (L₀, Q_rev), α/β, and cal/J units.
