## Notes / editorial decisions
- **Physiology deferred to Biology (this chapter's key call):** the source deck's ~7 'Fluids in Physiology' cards — arteries/veins/heart, blood-pressure flow, respiratory mechanics (inspiration/Boyle's-breathing, airway resistance, asthma, bronchi), and pulmonary surfactant — are anatomy/physiology the AAMC files as BIO, and they already live in the Biology Ch 6 Respiratory and Ch 7 Cardiovascular decks. This deck keeps only the general PHYSICS of flow (the resistance relations ΔP = QR and R ∝ ηL/r⁴, continuity, and Bernoulli), framed generally rather than blood-specific.
- **Thermodynamics contamination excluded:** the source deck's W = PΔV card is thermodynamic work and belongs to (and already lives in) Physics Ch 3 Thermodynamics; it was left out here.
- **Formula coverage:** ρ = m/V, F_g = ρVg, SG = ρ/ρ_water, P = F/A, P = P₀ + ρgh, F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂, F_b = ρ_fluid·V_displaced·g, A₁v₁ = A₂v₂, Q = Av, Poiseuille Q = πr⁴ΔP/(8ηL), and Bernoulli P + ½ρv² + ρgy = constant are all present.
- **AAMC-gap addition:** specific gravity (SG = ρ/ρ_water), an explicit AAMC bullet that was thin in the source summary.
- **Notation:** plain Unicode physics throughout — ρ, η, Δ, proper minus −, ½, and subscripts (P₀, A₁, r⁴, F_b).
