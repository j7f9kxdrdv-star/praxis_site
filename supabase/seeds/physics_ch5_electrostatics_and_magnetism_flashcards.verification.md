## Notes / editorial decisions
- **Notation fully rebuilt from a broken source (this chapter's main job):** the source Anki deck was riddled with ASCII math and LaTeX artifacts (k*(q1*q2/r2), \[V = U/q\], 10^9 Nm^2/C^2, mu0, sin(theta)) and a recurring 'magentic' typo. Every card was re-sourced from OpenStax into clean plain Unicode: F = kq₁q₂/r², E = kQ/r², U = kq₁q₂/r, V = kQ/r, τ = pE·sinθ, B = μ₀I/(2πr), F = qvB·sinθ, with e = 1.60×10⁻¹⁹ C and k = 8.99×10⁹ N·m²/C².
- **Circuits excluded (scope boundary):** current (I = ΔQ/Δt), EMF, resistance/Ohm's law, resistivity, capacitance, conductivity, and meters are AAMC 4C 'Circuit Elements' and belong to Physics Ch 6 Circuits. Only the definition of potential difference/voltage is kept here (electrostatics).
- **Magnetic materials framed by behavior, not electron configuration:** diamagnetic (paired electrons, weakly repelled), paramagnetic (unpaired, weakly attracted), ferromagnetic (aligned dipoles, strongly/permanently magnetic) — the Hund's-rule/orbital basis stays in chemistry electronic structure.
- **Formula coverage:** Coulomb's law, E = F/q and E = kQ/r², U = kq₁q₂/r, V = U/q = kQ/r, dipole moment p = qd and torque τ = pE·sinθ, the wire/loop fields (μ₀I/(2πr), μ₀I/(2r)), F = qvB·sinθ, F = ILB·sinθ, and the Lorentz force are all present.
- **Notation:** plain Unicode physics throughout — superscripts/subscripts (q₁, r², 10⁻¹⁹), θ/τ/μ₀, ×, ·, and proper minus −.
