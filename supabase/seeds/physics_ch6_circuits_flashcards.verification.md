## Notes / editorial decisions
- **Notation fully rebuilt from a LaTeX+HTML source (this chapter's main job):** the source Anki deck wrote every formula in LaTeX (\dfrac{Q}{\Delta t}, \rho\dfrac{L}{A}, \kappa C) and used HTML markup (<sub>, <div>, &nbsp;). Every card was re-sourced into clean plain Unicode: I = ΔQ/Δt, V = IR, R = ρL/A, σ = 1/ρ, P = IV = I²R = V²/R, R_eq = R₁ + R₂, 1/R_eq = 1/R₁ + 1/R₂, C = Q/V, C = ε₀A/d, U = ½CV² = Q²/(2C), V = ε − Ir. The assembler hard-flags any leftover LaTeX/HTML/caret-exponent/digit-underscore-subscript.
- **Capacitor vs resistor combination rule (the classic trap):** capacitors in SERIES combine as reciprocals (decrease) and in PARALLEL add directly (increase) — the exact REVERSE of resistors. Series/parallel and increase/decrease pairs are co-grouped/both-blanked so a card never telegraphs its own answer.
- **Scope boundaries enforced in prompts:** electrochemical-cell theory → Gen Chem Ch 12 (only the battery's emf/terminal voltage V = ε − Ir as a circuit driver is kept); nerve-impulse electrophysiology → Biology; magnetism + electrostatics fundamentals → Physics Ch 5. Voltage/potential-difference is framed here as the circuit driver.
- **Conduction:** metallic (free electrons in metallic bonds) vs electrolytic (mobile ions in solution) are co-grouped as a contrast; conductivity σ = 1/ρ in S/m included per the AAMC Conductivity bullet.
- **Temperature-of-resistance:** included as one LIGHT conceptual card (metal resistance rises with temperature); the α-coefficient formula was intentionally left out (beyond the concept summary + AAMC bullet).
