## Notes / editorial decisions
- **Notation rebuilt from an HTML/malformed source (this chapter's main job):** the source Anki wrote formulas in HTML markup (<sub>, <br>, &nbsp;, log₁₀) and its Doppler card was nested clozes-in-clozes. Every card was re-sourced into clean plain Unicode with FLAT clozes: v = fλ, ω = 2πf, T = 1/f, I = P/A, β = 10 log(I/I₀), I₀ = 1×10⁻¹² W/m², f_beat = |f₁ − f₂|, λ = 2L/n (string/open pipe), λ = 4L/n (closed pipe), f′ = f(v ± v_o)/(v ∓ v_s). The assembler hard-flags leftover HTML/LaTeX/caret-exponent/digit-underscore-subscript/trailing-'.,'.
- **Doppler sign convention stated explicitly:** the formula card names v (speed of sound), v_o (observer), v_s (source) and specifies the signs are chosen so that approach RAISES the frequency; a separate plain-language card gives approach → higher / recede → lower, with the matched pair co-grouped.
- **Scope boundaries enforced in prompts:** SHM mechanics (Hooke's law, spring/pendulum period) → Physics Ch 2 (already owns it); light-specific interference/diffraction/optics → Physics Ch 8 (general wave interference & standing waves kept here); ear/hearing physiology → Sensory (only ultrasound's medical use kept). Beats (f_beat = |f₁ − f₂|) kept as one light card (in the source Anki + standard MCAT interference content, adjacent to the explicit AAMC bullets).
- **Confusable pairs co-grouped/both-blanked:** transverse/longitudinal, crest/trough, node/antinode, constructive/destructive, in-phase/out-of-phase, approach/recede & higher/lower, open/closed pipe — so a card never telegraphs its own answer.
