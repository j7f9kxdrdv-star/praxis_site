## Notes / editorial decisions
- **Notation rebuilt from an HTML source (this chapter's main job):** the source Anki wrote formulas in HTML markup (<sub>, <br>, &nbsp;, &lt;) with a typo ("-I/o"). Every card was re-sourced into clean plain Unicode: c = fλ, n = c/v, n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂, sin θ_c = n₂/n₁, f = R/2, 1/f = 1/o + 1/i, m = −i/o, P = 1/f, d sin θ = mλ. The assembler hard-flags leftover HTML/LaTeX/caret-exponent/digit-underscore-subscript/"-I/o"/trailing-'.,'.
- **Sign conventions consolidated:** the source had ~15 heavily-overlapping sign cards. They were collapsed into a tight set using one consistent convention (real-is-positive): real image → i positive, inverted, m negative; virtual image → i negative, upright, m positive; converging element (convex lens / concave mirror) f positive, diverging element (concave lens / convex mirror) f negative; |m| > 1 enlarged, |m| < 1 reduced.
- **Quantum/photon side excluded (scope boundary):** photon energy E = hf, the photoelectric effect, atomic spectra, and blackbody radiation belong to Physics Ch 9 Atomic and Nuclear Phenomena (confirmed that deck owns them); only the qualitative energy ∝ frequency trend is kept for spectrum ordering. General mechanical-wave definitions → Ch 7 (applied to light here); eye anatomy → Sensory. The source's dubious 'complementary colour' wavelength card was dropped.
- **Confusable pairs co-grouped/both-blanked:** reflection/refraction, concave/convex, converging/diverging, real-inverted/virtual-upright, toward/away from the normal, higher-n/lower-n, positive/negative, constructive/destructive, bright-maxima/dark-minima, plane/circular polarization — so a card never telegraphs its own answer.
