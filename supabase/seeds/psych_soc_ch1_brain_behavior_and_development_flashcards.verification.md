## Disagreements with corpus

Eleven source-material claims (from the .docx) were transcription errors or factual confusions that were silently corrected before becoming cards.

1. **.docx "Neonephrine" (×4 occurrences)** — autocorrect from "norepinephrine." Corrected throughout (Cards 64, 65).

2. **.docx "Catacomb means all play important roles in the experiences of emotions"** — autocorrect from "Catecholamines all play important roles." Card 62 uses the correct term.

3. **.docx "Barocas area"** — autocorrect/typo for Broca's area. Cards 3, 51, 57 use correct spelling.

4. **.docx "biogenic a mean neurotransmitter"** — autocorrect from "biogenic amine." Card 66 uses the correct term.

5. **.docx "desiccated tissues" (neural-crest derivatives context)** — autocorrect from "diverse tissues" or "various tissues." Card 80 lists specific neural-crest derivatives per textbook.

6. **.docx "The cerebellum is divided into two halves called cerebral hemispheres"** — factually wrong. The *cerebrum* has cerebral hemispheres; the cerebellum has its own cerebellar hemispheres. Cards in §15-16 correctly attribute the hemispheres to the cerebrum/brain.

7. **.docx "central hemispheres commute contralaterally" / "central hemispheres communicate"** — wrong words (cerebral, not central; communicate, not commute). Card 56 uses correct terminology.

8. **.docx "An oxytocin"** — typo from "and oxytocin." Card 40 lists both posterior pituitary hormones correctly.

9. **.docx "post-central nervous system" (glycine context)** — wrong location. Glycine is found in the central nervous system, specifically the brainstem and spinal cord (not "post-central NS," which is not a recognized structure). Card 67 uses the correct anatomy.

10. **.docx "reflect behavior"** — autocorrect from "reflex behavior." Card 7 uses correct term.

11. **.docx multiple sentence fragments** (e.g., "studied how the mind adapts to" cut off, "behavior deficits of people with brain" cut off) — incomplete source phrasing; cards were composed from complete textbook framing rather than the truncated .docx fragments.
