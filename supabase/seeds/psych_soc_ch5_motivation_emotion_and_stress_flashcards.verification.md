## Notes / editorial decisions
- **Photo-degraded source summary — gaps CLOSED, not skipped.** The concept summary was glare-obscured over the General Adaptation Syndrome stages and the stress-management techniques. These were rebuilt from OpenStax + AAMC + the Anki deck (Anki card #30 supplied the full alarm→resistance→exhaustion set), so nothing was dropped for being illegible.
- **Cross-deck dedup (this chapter's key call):** the brain-structure roster the source summary listed under Emotion — the limbic system, amygdala (location/general fear-rage), thalamus (relay), hypothalamus (hunger/satiety centers), hippocampus, and prefrontal cortex — is fully owned by Ch 1 and was NOT rebuilt here. This deck keeps only the emotion-EXPERIENCE physiology the theories depend on: autonomic/sympathetic arousal, the physiological markers of emotion, and a light note that the amygdala tags emotional/fear salience. That is dedup, not omission — the anatomy lives in Ch 1.
- **Over-blanked source cards split** — Maslow's five levels, the three GAS stages, the four motivation factors, and self-determination's three needs were broken out of single crammed groups.
- **Matched-pair leak guarding** — confusable pairs (extrinsic/intrinsic, primary/secondary drives, distress/eustress, primary/secondary appraisal, problem-focused/emotion-focused coping, and the three emotion theories) are co-grouped or blanked on both sides.
- **AAMC-gap additions** — hunger/eating and substance addiction as biological motivators, the adaptive role of emotion, stressor types (cataclysmic vs personal vs daily hassles), stress outcomes (physiological/emotional/behavioral), and the problem- vs emotion-focused coping distinction.
- **Universal-emotions count** — taught as Ekman's six (happiness, sadness, fear, anger, disgust, surprise), noting some lists add contempt for seven, since the source summary (7), Anki (5), and AAMC (7 incl. joy) disagreed.
