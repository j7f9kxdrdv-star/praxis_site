## Notes / editorial decisions
- **Contamination excluded** — the source Anki deck included a junk 'Cloze Card' stub, which was left out.
- **DSM-precision focus** — this chapter's failure mode is confusing near-identical disorders, so each card was verified against the DISTINGUISHING criterion (Bipolar I = manic episode vs Bipolar II = MDE + hypomanic; MDD ≥2-week episode vs persistent depressive ≥2 years; obsessions [thoughts] vs compulsions [acts]; depersonalization [self] vs derealization [surroundings]; OCPD ≠ OCD; illness anxiety [minimal symptoms] vs somatic symptom disorder vs conversion [neuro, no cause]).
- **Biological-bases dedup (this chapter's key call):** Ch 1 already teaches, as neurotransmitter-function cards, the schizophrenia dopamine hypothesis, the Parkinson's dopamine-loss mechanism, Alzheimer's cholinergic loss, and low-serotonin-in-depression. This deck keeps concise, DISORDER-framed biology for the AAMC-listed disorders — Alzheimer's β-amyloid plaques + tau tangles (new), depression's glucocorticoid + monoamine profile, bipolar's high NE/serotonin, and schizophrenia's risk factors — and just ONE light Parkinson's card (substantia nigra), rather than repeating Ch 1's neurotransmitter-mechanism cards.
- **Over-blanked source cards split** — PTSD's symptom clusters, Parkinson's signs, and several multi-symptom personality-disorder cards were broken into single-concept cards.
- **Matched-pair leak guarding** — positive/negative symptoms, biomedical/biopsychosocial, mania/hypomania, Bipolar I/II, obsessions/compulsions, depersonalization/derealization, and the three PD clusters are co-grouped or blanked together so one side never gives away the other.
- **Cross-deck boundaries:** general neurotransmitter FUNCTION and brain anatomy stay in Ch 1; the stress response/GAS stays in Ch 5; personality theory/traits stay in Ch 6 (the personality DISORDERS live here).
- **Coverage audit** — the final card set was cross-checked against the full AAMC 7A Psychological Disorders checklist (understanding, every disorder type, and the biological bases) to confirm every listed sub-topic is represented.
- **Leak red-team + originality + cloze_count** verified per-card by an adversarial pass (see workflow transcript).
