## Notes / editorial decisions
- **Scoped by exclusion — three source-deck topics live in other decks and were dropped here:** groupthink (Ch 8 Social Processes), the reference group (Ch 6 Identity, as the self-comparison group), and symbolic interactionism (Ch 11 owns it as a sociological paradigm). The wider 'social thinking' content a reader might expect — attribution, prejudice, stereotypes, ethnocentrism, self-fulfilling prophecy, discrimination — is fully owned by Ch 10 Social Thinking and is not duplicated here.
- **Animal-communication trivia consolidated** — the source deck's five single-animal example cards (dogs, peacocks, insects, birds) were replaced with one card on the modalities of animal communication.
- **A kept distinction** — the social-construction MODEL OF EMOTION (emotions arise from social/situational context) is retained here and is deliberately NOT conflated with Ch 11's social-constructionism sociological paradigm.
- **Two source textbooks** — the social-structure cards (status, role, groups, Gemeinschaft/Gesellschaft, networks) were sourced from OpenStax Sociology 3e; the self-presentation and communication cards from OpenStax Psychology 2e.
- **Matched-pair leak guarding** — ascribed/achieved status, role conflict/role strain, in-group/out-group, primary/secondary group, Gemeinschaft/Gesellschaft, the basic vs social-construction emotion models, front/back stage, and verbal/nonverbal communication are each blanked so one member never gives away the other.
- **Coverage audit** — the final card set was cross-checked against the AAMC 10A (status, role, groups) + 8B (self-presentation, communication) checklist to confirm every listed concept is represented.
- **Leak red-team + originality + cloze_count** verified per-card by an adversarial pass (see workflow transcript).
